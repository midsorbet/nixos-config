# Facts

Identify the subject from the recent discussion and whether the request concerns planned or completed work. Give only the facts needed to understand what will happen or what changed, including important implications for development, existing or future system behavior, and end use.

Use terse, direct, jargon-free language for a capable engineer who is new to the subject. Distinguish established facts, plans, and inferences when that difference matters. Do not turn the response into a broad fact-finding exercise or an exhaustive inventory.
